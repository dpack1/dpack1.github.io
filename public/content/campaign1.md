# [City of Neverwinter](!https://www.aidedd.org/atlas/index.php?map=N&l=1) – [Map](NeverWinter.jpg)
“Jewel of the North” - Prosperous northern coastal city of trade and craft. Regarded by some as the most cosmopolitan and civilized city in all of Faerun. Some attribute the name to Neverwinter’s gardens growing even through winter. Neverwinter river, that flows through the city, is perpetually warm.

### [City of Waterdeep](https://www.aidedd.org/atlas/index.php?map=W&l=1) -  [Map](WaterDeep.png)

“City of Splendors” “Crown of the North” - trade hub between the mineral-rich north and the merchant kingdoms of Amn and Calimshan to the south (east and west as well). Named for its deep water harbor.


## Phandalin – [Map](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/forgottenrealms/images/c/c8/Phandalin-Background/revision/latest?cb=20220416050438)
**Summary** - Mainly human village near the Triboar trail. Used to be a farming community that, years ago, was raided by orcs and left abandoned in ruins. The past several years have seen a resurgence in activity as people flock to rebuild and repopulate. The town’s still small, but many see opportunity in it.

**History** - Human village. Abandoned in 951 DR after an orcs raid, settlers from Neverwinter and Waterdeep had resettled Phandalin. It became a frontier settlement of farmers and prospectors looking for wealth in the Sword Mountains.

### [Order of the Gauntlet](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_the_Gauntlet) – 
A coalition of morally upstanding and often devout warriors, knights, paladins, and clerics who dedicate themselves to the destruction of evil. They believe it justified to punish an act of evil, not a thought or feeling.

### [The Redbrands](https://crossgoersdnd.fandom.com/wiki/The_Red_Brands) – 
A primarily human group of bandits and brigands. Many are seen wearing tattered red cloaks.

### The Harpers – 
Semi-secret organization of adventurers and spies dedicated to promoting equality, preserving history, maintaining a balance between civilization and nature, and opposing abuse of power. Most members happen to be druids or rangers but the wider their reach of influence the better. Symbol of a silver harp between the horns of a crescent moon

### Lords’ Alliance - “Council of Lords” - 
A partnership of allied political powers concerned with mutual security and prosperity. Originally formed to oppose the growing influence of thieves’ guilds. They are proud and make their presence known whenever they can as a symbol of peace and order. Symbol is a golden or white crown on a red background

### The Emerald Enclave – 
Organization of druids and other nature-worshippers who avoid good and evil to focus on the needs of the natural world. They prefer to act with civilized folk to promote the health of the natural world without resorting to acts of violence.

### The Red Wizards – 
Notorious and nefarious magocratic ruling class of the harsh country of Thay. Historically they’re slavers, demonologists, and arcane experimenters. Each wizard is an autonomous entity and usually pursues their own selfish goals.

### The Zhentarim “the Black Network” - 
Old organization of self-serving thieves, spies, assassins, and wizards. They aim to gain influence and power primarily through controlling the economy, often by dominating the market of protection services in the area. They will go to great lengths to eliminate competitors and secure future profits.  
Symbol is a winged wyrm on a yellow background diving down with its mouth open toward a circle
